Scroll

I know the girl who started dancing at three

and never agreed to stop.

The seven-year-old awake past midnight with a book,

promising herself she would stay like this.

I know the teacher who keeps every small gift

in the school bag she carried at five,

who says her true mission is to love

and means it.

I know you were born filled to the brim with paradox

and that sometimes you overflow.

I know some days sit heavy on your heart.

I’m all for that.

An idea doesn’t clean the whole house

and put brownies in the oven.

An idea doesn’t stop on the walk to the train

because the sky looked bruised,

and ask if it was as beautiful where I am.

It was.

So this is what I’m trying to say:

it was never the idea.

It’s you. All of you.

The brim, and the overflow.

Never stop being like this.

Happy birthday, Emma.

I would have brought you flowers,

but I can’t.

So I grew you these.
